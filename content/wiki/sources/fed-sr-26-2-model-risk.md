- **What:** supervisory letter (with attached guidance PDF)
- **Publisher:** Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, jointly with the OCC and FDIC
- **Date:** 2026-04-17
- **Original:** [SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm)

## Summary

Announces the revised interagency model risk management (MRM) guidance and lists what it supersedes.

## Key claims

- Rescinds SR 11-7 (2011-04-04), the long-standing basis of bank MRM, and SR 21-8 (the 2021 BSA/AML model-risk statement).
- Expected to be most relevant to Fed-supervised banking organizations with more than $30 billion in total assets.
- Practices should be risk-based and tailored to the institution's model risk profile and the size and complexity of its operations.
