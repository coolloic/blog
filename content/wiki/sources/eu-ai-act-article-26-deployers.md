## Summary

Article 26 is the main article for enterprises that deploy a vendor's high-risk system. It applies from 2027-12-02 for Annex III systems and from 2028-08-02 for Annex I systems.

## Key claims

- Use the system according to the provider's instructions, through suitable technical and organisational measures.
- Assign human oversight to people with the competence, training, authority and support to do it.
- Where the deployer controls the input data, ensure it is relevant and sufficiently representative.
- Monitor operation. Inform the provider and authorities of risks, suspend use when needed, and report serious incidents.
- Keep the automatically generated logs under your control for an appropriate period of at least six months.
- Inform workers' representatives and affected workers before workplace use. Inform people subject to Annex III decisions.
- Use the provider's Article 13 information to carry out the GDPR data-protection impact assessment.
