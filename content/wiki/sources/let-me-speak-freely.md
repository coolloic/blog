## Summary

Empirical study of whether forcing outputs into formats like JSON or XML changes what LLMs can do. Compares free-form generation with format-restricted variants on reasoning and knowledge tasks.

## Key claims

- Format restrictions produce a significant drop in reasoning ability on the tested tasks.
- Stricter constraints tend to cause larger degradation.
- The effect is task-dependent: reasoning suffers most.
