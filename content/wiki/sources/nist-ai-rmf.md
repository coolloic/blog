- **What:** official programme page
- **Publisher:** US National Institute of Standards and Technology
- **Date:** accessed 2026-11-14
- **Original:** [AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) (framework text: NIST AI 100-1)

## Summary

The entry point to NIST's AI risk guidance and the resources around it.

## Key claims

- AI RMF 1.0 was released 2023-01-26 after a public consensus process. Use is voluntary.
- Its core is organized into four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
- Companion resources: the AI RMF Playbook, a roadmap, crosswalks to other frameworks, and the Trustworthy and Responsible AI Resource Center (launched 2023-03-30).
- The Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1) was released 2024-07-26.
- A concept note for a critical-infrastructure profile was released 2026-04-07.
- The page says AI RMF 1.0 is being revised under the White House AI Action Plan; no revision date is given.
