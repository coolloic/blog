## Summary

Contrasts the three SOC report families and explains how a customer obtains them.

## Key claims

- SOC 1 covers controls relevant to customers' financial reporting (SSAE 18 / ISAE 3402). SOC 2 is an audit against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. SOC 3 is a public summary of the SOC 2 without internal detail.
- SOC 1 and SOC 2 reports require an NDA and are downloaded from AWS Artifact; SOC 3 is public.
- AWS issues SOC 2 twice a year, each report covering 12 months, about 9-10 weeks after period end. Monthly bridge letters cover the gap.
