## Summary

Anthropic's commercial terms for API customers cover ownership of inputs and outputs, and use restrictions.

## Key claims

- Section B: the customer owns Outputs. Anthropic assigns any rights it has in Outputs to the customer, subject to compliance with the terms.
- Section D.4: customers may not use the Services to build a competing product or service, including to train competing AI models. They also may not reverse engineer the Services, or resell them without approval.
