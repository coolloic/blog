## Summary

Measures forgetting of domain knowledge, reasoning and reading comprehension in 1B–7B models during continual instruction tuning.

## Key claims

- Catastrophic forgetting appears across 1B–7B models.
- In that range, forgetting got more severe as scale increased.
- Decoder-only BLOOMZ forgot less than encoder-decoder mT0.
- General instruction tuning beforehand helps mitigate later forgetting.
