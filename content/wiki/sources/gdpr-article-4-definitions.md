## Summary

Definitions that drive every privacy decision in an LLM pipeline. The key point for engineers: pseudonymised data is still personal data, because it can be re-linked with the separately held key.

## Key claims

- **Personal data (4(1)):** any information relating to an identified or identifiable living person, directly or indirectly, e.g. via name, ID number, location, online identifier or physical, economic, cultural or social traits.
- **Processing (4(2)):** any operation on personal data, automated or not: collecting, storing, organising, altering, retrieving, using, disclosing, combining, restricting, erasing, destroying.
- **Pseudonymisation (4(5)):** processing so that data can no longer be attributed to a person without additional information that is kept separately under technical and organisational safeguards.
- **Processor (4(8)):** a party that processes personal data on behalf of the controller.
