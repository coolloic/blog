## Summary

Describes what the Gemini API (and AI Studio) keeps for policy enforcement and how flagged traffic is reviewed and acted on.

## Key claims

- Prompts, accompanying context and generated outputs are retained for 55 days to detect and prevent Prohibited Use Policy violations and for required legal disclosures.
- Flagged content can be assessed by authorized Google staff through an internal review tool; projects with persistent suspicious activity can also be flagged for manual review.
- Logged data is used only for policy enforcement, not to train models other than enforcement models.
- Enforcement escalates from contact to temporary limits, suspension and, last, account closure, with an appeal link.
- The page states no exemption or opt-out and does not distinguish paid from unpaid use.
