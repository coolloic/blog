## Summary

Summarises the AI part of the EU's Digital Omnibus package. The Commission proposed it in late 2025, Parliament and Council reached a provisional deal in May 2026, and it was published as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

## Key claims

- Published in the Official Journal 2026-07-24; in force 2026-07-27.
- High-risk application dates: stand-alone Annex III systems move from 2026-08-02 to 2027-12-02; Annex I product-embedded systems to 2028-08-02.
- Article 4 AI literacy is softened: organizations must take measures to support staff literacy but no longer guarantee a sufficient level for each person. Article 26(2) oversight training for high-risk deployers remains.
- Article 50 transparency applies from 2026-08-02. Generative systems already on the market have until 2026-12-02 for machine-readable marking of outputs.
- New prohibitions on "nudifier" systems and AI-generated CSAM.
- Systems that providers self-assess as not high-risk under Art. 6(3) must still be registered, with simplified information.
- The legal basis for processing special-category data to detect bias now covers all AI systems, under a strict-necessity test.
- National regulatory sandboxes are due by 2027-08-02. SME relief is extended to small mid-caps.
