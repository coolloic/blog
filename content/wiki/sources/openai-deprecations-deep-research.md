## Summary

Lists retired models with notice dates, shutdown dates and replacements. This page records only the entries relevant to search and deep research.

## Key claims

- `o3-deep-research` and `o4-mini-deep-research` (and their 2025-06-26 snapshots): notice 2026-04-22, shutdown 2026-07-23, replacement `gpt-5.6-sol`.
- `gpt-4o-search-preview-2025-03-11` and `gpt-4o-mini-search-preview-2025-03-11`: same dates, replacement `gpt-5.6-terra`. (The page lists the dated snapshots, not an undated `gpt-4o-search-preview` alias.)
