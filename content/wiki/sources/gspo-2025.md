## Summary

GSPO argues that GRPO's per-token importance ratios make training unstable. It computes the importance ratio from the likelihood of the whole sequence, and it clips and optimises at the sequence level.

## Key claims

- Importance ratio, clipping and optimisation all happen at the sequence level, not per token.
- More stable and efficient than GRPO, and notably stabilises RL on mixture-of-experts models.
- Credited with contributing to the gains in the latest Qwen3 models.
