## Summary

Explains Google Cloud's shared-responsibility model for HIPAA and lists the products covered by its BAA.

## Key claims

- The BAA is reviewed and accepted through the Cloud console's privacy-compliance flow; terms live at cloud.google.com/terms/hipaa-baa.
- Covered products include Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Generative AI on Agent Platform, Agent Search, Gemini Enterprise, Model Armor and Conversational Agents, among others.
- Pre-GA offerings must not be used with PHI unless their terms say otherwise; services not covered must be disabled, or not used for PHI.
- Product notes: use regional APIs and locations for Agent Search with PHI; keep PHI and credentials out of Conversational Agents definitions.
- The page does not mention the Gemini Developer API or AI Studio.
