## Summary

Estimates the stock of publicly available human-written text and projects dataset growth for frontier models. If trends continue, training sets reach the size of that stock within a few years, making data rather than compute the binding constraint.

## Key claims

- Frontier models would be trained on datasets about the size of the public human text stock between 2026 and 2032.
- Over-training (more tokens per parameter than compute-optimal) moves that date earlier.
- Synthetic data, transfer from other modalities and better data efficiency are discussed as ways to keep scaling.
