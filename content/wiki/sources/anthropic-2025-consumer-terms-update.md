## Summary

Illustrates the consumer vs commercial split that matters for enterprise privacy: consumer chat data may be used for training (by user choice), while commercial-terms products are excluded.

## Key claims

- Free, Pro and Max users (including Claude Code on those accounts) choose whether their data is used to improve models; the setting can be changed later.
- Opting in extends retention to five years; otherwise the 30-day retention applies. Deleted conversations are not used for future training.
- Not affected: Claude for Work (Team, Enterprise), the API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, Claude Gov, Claude for Education.
- Existing users had until 2025-10-08 to choose.
