## Summary

The authoritative list of AWS services covered by the AWS Business Associate Addendum.

## Key claims

- Customers must sign the AWS BAA before using any listed service with protected health information.
- Amazon Bedrock is listed, and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore is listed separately; no exclusions are noted for either.
- Generally available features of a listed service are eligible unless specifically excluded.
- Customers remain responsible for configuring services in a HIPAA-consistent way.
