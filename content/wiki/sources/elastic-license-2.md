## Summary

ELv2 grants broad rights to use, copy, modify and redistribute software, subject to three limitations. Tools under it, such as Arize Phoenix, can be self-hosted internally at no cost but are not OSI open source.

## Key claims

- You may not offer the software to third parties as a hosted or managed service that exposes a substantial set of its features.
- You may not move, change, disable or circumvent licence-key functionality, or remove functionality it protects.
- You may not alter, remove or obscure the licensor's licensing, copyright or other notices.
