## Summary

OpenAI agreed to acquire Promptfoo, the eval and red-teaming startup, to add security testing to OpenAI Frontier, its enterprise platform for agents. Deal terms were not disclosed and the deal was subject to closing conditions; no closing announcement was found as of 2026-11-14.

## Key claims

- Promptfoo was founded in 2024 by Ian Webster and Michael D'Angelo; it had raised about $23M.
- OpenAI said it would keep building the open-source Promptfoo interface and library.
- Planned uses inside Frontier: automated red-teaming, security evaluation of agent workflows, monitoring agent activity for risk and compliance.
- Promptfoo says more than 25% of Fortune 500 companies use its products (company claim).
