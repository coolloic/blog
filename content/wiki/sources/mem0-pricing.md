## Summary

Pricing is metered per request type, not per stored memory or token.

## Key claims

- Free: unlimited end users; 10,000 adds and 1,000 retrievals per month for the first 30 days on Mem0's model calls; afterwards bring your own LLM key, with 50,000 adds and 10,000 retrievals per month; usage-based overage with a user-set monthly cap; community support.
- Custom (contact sales): volume-based limits plus graph memory (entity linking), user profiles, Dream consolidation, analytics, SSO, audit logs, SLA, on-prem deployment, private Slack support.
- Retrievals are priced as the scarce resource (ten times fewer than adds on the free tier).
