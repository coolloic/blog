## Summary

A dated checklist of the Act's staged application. The current version already includes the high-risk delays from the Digital Omnibus.

## Key claims

- 2024-07-12: published in the Official Journal. 2024-08-01: entry into force.
- 2025-02-02: prohibited practices and AI-literacy duties apply.
- 2025-08-02: GPAI model obligations, governance, notified bodies and most penalty provisions apply. GPAI models already on the market have until 2027-08-02.
- 2026-08-02: the remainder of the Act applies unless stated otherwise, including the Article 50 transparency duties. Generative systems already on the market have until 2026-12-02 for Article 50(2) machine-readable marking.
- 2026-12-02: new prohibitions on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM apply.
- 2027-12-02: Chapter III obligations for Annex III (stand-alone) high-risk systems. 2028-08-02: Annex I (product-embedded) high-risk systems.
- 2030-08-02: high-risk systems used by public authorities must comply.
