## Summary

Chatbot Arena ranks models from crowdsourced pairwise comparisons: users chat with two anonymous models side by side and vote for the better answer, and the votes are fitted to a statistical ranking.

## Key claims

- Pairwise human preference on live, user-written prompts avoids a fixed test set.
- More than 240K votes had been collected at the time of writing.
- Crowdsourced votes agree well with expert raters.
