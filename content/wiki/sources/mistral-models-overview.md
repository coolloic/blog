## Summary

Lists Mistral's generalist and specialist models (OCR, speech, code, embeddings, moderation, time series), their licences and lifecycle dates. It also hosts third-party open-weight models (GLM, DeepSeek).

## Key claims

- Mistral Large 4 (v26.10) is open-weight under the Mistral Ecosystem Open Weight License; Mistral Small 4 (v26.03) and Ministral 3 (3B/8B/14B) are Apache 2.0.
- Several specialist models (OCR, Codestral, Voxtral transcription, embeddings) are premier, API-only.
- Mistral Large 3 is deprecated as of 2026-10-31 and retires 2027-04-30, replaced by Large 4.
- The platform also serves third-party open-weight models such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Z.ai GLM 5.3 (MIT).
