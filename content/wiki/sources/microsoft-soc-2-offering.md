## Summary

A practical account of what a SOC 2 report is and how to read one, written from a cloud provider's side.

## Key claims

- SOC reports are AICPA internal-control reports that help users assess the risk of an outsourced service. SOC 2 is performed under SSAE 18 against the 2017 Trust Services Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy.
- An independent CPA firm gives an opinion on the fairness of the system description, the suitability of control design, and, for Type 2, operating effectiveness over a specified period.
- Type 1 does not look back over a period of performance. Type 2 covers a period, which Microsoft runs as a rolling 12-month window.
- Bridge (gap) letters are management self-attestations covering the time since the last audited period. They are not audit opinions.
- Exceptions and management responses appear near the end of the report. "User entity responsibilities" are the controls the customer must operate for the overall system to meet the criteria.
- Downloading reports requires a subscription or trial account, as AICPA requires.
