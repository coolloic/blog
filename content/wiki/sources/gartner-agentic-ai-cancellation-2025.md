## Summary

Forecasts that a large share of agentic AI projects will be canceled and attributes this to cost, unclear value and weak risk controls, compounded by vendor hype. Read via news coverage of the release.

## Key claims

- Over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027.
- Causes: escalating costs, unclear business value, inadequate risk controls.
- "Agent washing": vendors rebrand assistants, RPA and chatbots as agents; Gartner estimates only about 130 of thousands of agentic vendors are genuine.
- A January 2025 Gartner poll of 3,412 webinar attendees found only 19% of organisations had made significant agentic AI investments (42% conservative, 31% wait-and-see, 8% none); a webinar poll is not a representative sample.
- Advice: pursue agentic AI only where it delivers clear value or ROI.
