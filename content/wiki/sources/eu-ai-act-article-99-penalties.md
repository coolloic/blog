## Summary

Sets maximum administrative fines as the higher of a fixed amount or a share of total worldwide annual turnover, with a lower-of rule for SMEs.

## Key claims

- Prohibited practices (Article 5): up to EUR 35 million or 7% of turnover.
- Most other operator obligations, including provider (Art. 16), deployer (Art. 26) and transparency (Art. 50) duties: up to EUR 15 million or 3%.
- Incorrect, incomplete or misleading information to authorities: up to EUR 7.5 million or 1%.
- For SMEs and start-ups the cap is whichever amount is lower. As amended (new paragraph 6a), the lower-of rule also covers small mid-caps for the EUR 15M/3% and EUR 7.5M/1% tiers, but not for prohibited practices.
