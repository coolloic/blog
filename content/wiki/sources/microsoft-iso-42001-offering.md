## Summary

Defines an AI management system (AIMS) and lists the Microsoft services that have independent ISO/IEC 42001 certification.

## Key claims

- ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AIMS. It is meant for organizations that provide or use AI products and services.
- An AIMS is the set of organizational policies, objectives and processes for responsible development, provision or use of AI.
- In-scope services (as listed 2026-11): GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Chat, Copilot Health, Copilot Studio, Dragon Copilot (including Radiologist), Microsoft Foundry and Security Copilot. Certificates and audit reports are on the Service Trust Portal.
- Customers can rely on Microsoft's certificate in their own assessment, but must still have an assessor evaluate their own controls and implementation.
