- **Original:** [Gemini API pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing)

## Summary

Paid-tier per-token prices for each Gemini model, with discounted Batch/Flex and premium Priority tiers, context caching rates and the free tier.

## Key claims

- Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon: $2/$10 per MTok, rising to $4/$20 on 2026-12-01; Hydrogen $1.50/$7.50.
- Gemini 3.8/3.6 Flash $0.75/$3.75 rising to $1.50/$7.50 on 2027-01-01; 3.5 Flash-Lite $0.30/$2.50.
- Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview has prompt-length tiers: $2/$12 up to 200K, $4/$18 above.
- Batch/Flex halve prices; Priority is about 1.8x.
- Cached input is 0.05x of base on Gemini 4 (e.g. Carbon $0.10, Hydrogen $0.075) and 0.1x on 3.8 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.1 Pro, which also bill cache storage per hour.
- Output prices include thinking tokens.
- Hydrogen, Flash and Flash-Lite have a free tier whose data may be used to improve Google products; Carbon, Argon and 3.1 Pro do not.
- Search grounding: 5,000 free requests/month then $14 per 1,000.
