## Summary

The landing page positions SGLang as a high-performance serving framework built around RadixAttention and prefix caching, scaling from one GPU to large clusters.

## Key claims

- Supports NVIDIA, AMD, Intel Xeon, Google TPU, Ascend NPU and Moore Threads accelerators.
- OpenAI- and Hugging Face-compatible APIs; models such as Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek.
- Self-reported: trillions of tokens per day and more than 400,000 GPUs in deployment; integrated with RL training frameworks.
