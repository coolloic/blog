## Summary

Explains AWS's certification against ISO/IEC 42001 and what it does and does not mean for customers.

## Key claims

- AWS's certificate was issued by an ANAB-accredited certification body (Schellman), so it is recognized across IAF member countries. It covers a defined scope of services listed on the certificate, which is available in AWS Artifact.
- Customers are not certified by association; the provider's certificate can make their own certification easier.
- The standard's text must be bought from ISO; AWS cannot share it.
