## Summary

Walks clause by clause through what certification audits check. Use it as orientation only; other secondary sources number Annex A differently, so check against the licensed standard.

## Key claims

- The certifiable requirements are in clauses 4-10: context and scope (4), leadership and AI policy (5), planning, including risk assessment and treatment, the Statement of Applicability and objectives (6), support: resources, competence, awareness, documented information (7), operation (8), performance evaluation: monitoring, internal audit, management review (9), improvement (10).
- Clause 6.1.4 requires a process for AI system impact assessment and clause 8.4 requires carrying it out and keeping results. ISO 27001 has no equivalent requirement.
- Annex A holds 38 reference controls grouped under nine objectives (A.2-A.10), covering AI policy, impact assessment, the AI system life cycle and data governance.
- The Statement of Applicability records which Annex A controls apply and why. Annex A is a catalogue to choose from, not a mandatory checklist.
