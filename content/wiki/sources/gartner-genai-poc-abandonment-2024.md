## Summary

Forecasts a high abandonment rate for GenAI projects at the POC stage and names the causes. Read via syndicated copies of the release.

## Key claims

- At least 30% of GenAI projects will be abandoned after POC by the end of 2025.
- Causes: poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs, unclear business value.
- Some deployment approaches cost US$5M-20M up front.
- Executives want returns quickly, while organisations struggle to prove and realise value.
