## Summary

Reviews the evolution from lexical to semantic to LLM-based evaluation metrics and finds the same validation problems at every stage.

## Key claims

- No single automatic metric has become definitive; each captures only some aspects of quality.
- Metric effectiveness varies by task and dataset; correlations with human judgment are inconsistent and validation practice is unstructured.
- These problems persist for LLM-as-a-judge and for RAG evaluation.
- Recommendation: choose metrics for the task, combine complementary evaluations, and invest in better validation.
