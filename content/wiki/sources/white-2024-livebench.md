## Summary

Limits contamination by time: questions come from recent math competitions, arXiv papers, news and datasets, and are refreshed monthly. Answers are scored automatically against ground truth, avoiding both LLM-judge bias and crowd preferences.

## Key claims

- Categories: math, coding, reasoning, language, instruction following, data analysis.
- Includes harder, contamination-limited versions of Big-Bench Hard, AMPS and IFEval tasks.
- Monthly question refresh; harder tasks added over time.
- Objective scoring avoids judge errors on hard questions.
