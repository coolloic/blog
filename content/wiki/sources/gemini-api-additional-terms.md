## Summary

The contractual layer under the Gemini Developer API. It splits the service into Unpaid and Paid Services with very different data use, which makes billing status the main privacy control on this surface.

## Key claims

- Unpaid Services (AI Studio, free quota): inputs and outputs may be used to improve Google products and ML, and human reviewers may read them after they are disconnected from the account, key and project. Google tells users not to submit sensitive, confidential or personal data.
- Paid Services: prompts and responses are not used to improve products, and processing falls under the Data Processing Addendum for products where Google is a processor.
- Paid Services prompts and responses are logged for a limited period for abuse detection, safety and legal disclosure, and may be stored transiently or cached in any country where Google or its agents have facilities.
- Grounding with Google Search and with Google Maps stores prompts, context and outputs for 30 days.
- Apps made available to users in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK may use only Paid Services.
