## Summary

The original LLM Top 10, written in 2023 by a small group of security practitioners and revised as v1.1. It framed LLM risk largely in terms of the model and its plugins. The project page says the community has since grown to more than 600 contributing experts from over 18 countries.

## Key claims

- **v1.1 order:** LLM01 Prompt Injection, LLM02 Insecure Output Handling, LLM03 Training Data Poisoning, LLM04 Model Denial of Service, LLM05 Supply Chain Vulnerabilities, LLM06 Sensitive Information Disclosure, LLM07 Insecure Plugin Design, LLM08 Excessive Agency, LLM09 Overreliance, LLM10 Model Theft.
- The project now sits under the broader OWASP GenAI Security Project, which publishes more than the Top 10.
