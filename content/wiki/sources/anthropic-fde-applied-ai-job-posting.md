## Summary

Describes an embedded, customer-facing engineering role with Anthropic's most strategic enterprise customers, framed as one of the founding FDE positions. Useful as a concrete statement of what an LLM-lab FDE ships.

## Key claims

- Build production applications with Claude inside customer systems, and support deployment of Anthropic products in enterprise settings.
- Named deliverables: MCP servers, sub-agents and agent skills for production workflows.
- Identify and document repeatable deployment patterns and share them with Product and Engineering; work alongside post-sales, product and engineering teams.
- Find new deployment opportunities during engagements and represent Anthropic in the field.
- Qualifications: 3+ years in a technical customer-facing role (FDE, or software engineering with consulting); production LLM experience (prompt engineering, agents, evaluation frameworks, scaled deployment); strong Python; comfort with ambiguity; communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders. Vertical experience (financial services, healthcare/life sciences) is a plus.
- About 25% travel to customer sites; base salary $200k-$300k (this US listing, as of the posting).
- Caveat: other Anthropic FDE Applied AI listings seen on job-board mirrors give different terms (e.g. $280k-$320k for some US locations, GBP 225k-255k in London, and 25-50% travel for a Federal Civilian variant); check anthropic.com/careers for current terms.
