## Summary

Systematises the deep research agent space and its open problems. Read at abstract level.

## Key claims

- Defines deep research agents as LLM-based systems that combine dynamic reasoning, long-horizon planning, multi-hop retrieval, iterative tool use and structured report generation.
- Taxonomy axes: static versus dynamic workflows; single-agent versus multi-agent; planning strategy; API-based retrieval versus browser-based exploration.
- Also covers tool frameworks: code execution, multimodal input, MCP.
- Challenges: limited access to external knowledge, inefficiency of sequential execution, and evaluation metrics and benchmarks misaligned with practical goals.
