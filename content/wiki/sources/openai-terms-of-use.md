Third-party summaries say it keeps the competing-models clause, but that version was not read directly. A clause of the same kind has appeared since at least the March 2023 terms.
- **Original:** [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/)

## Summary

OpenAI's terms assign ownership of Output to the user, and list prohibited uses of the services.

## Key claims

- Between the user and OpenAI, and to the extent the law permits, the user owns Output. OpenAI assigns its rights in Output to the user.
- Prohibited: using Output to develop models that compete with OpenAI.
- Output may not be unique: other users can receive similar Output.
