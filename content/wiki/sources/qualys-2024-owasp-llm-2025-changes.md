## Summary

Explains the 2023 to 2025 transition: the list was finalised in late 2024 and designated 2025. It describes two new entries and several reworked ones, with reasons drawn from incidents and community concern.

## Key claims

- Prompt Injection stays first; Sensitive Information Disclosure rises to #2; Supply Chain rises to #3.
- Training Data Poisoning broadens into Data and Model Poisoning; output handling drops slightly in rank.
- New: System Prompt Leakage (incidents showed prompt contents leak) and Vector and Embedding Weaknesses (RAG is now central).
- Misinformation is expanded to address Overreliance; Unbounded Consumption replaces Denial of Service and adds cost management.
- Excessive Agency is expanded because agentic architectures give models autonomy and permissions.
