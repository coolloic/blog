## Summary

CUA combines GPT-4o vision with reasoning trained by reinforcement learning to operate GUIs from pixels, without OS- or site-specific APIs. It powered the Operator research preview.

## Key claims

- OSWorld 38.1% (prior SOTA 22.0%, human 72.4%); WebArena 58.1% (human 78.2%); WebVoyager 87%.
- WebVoyager tasks are relatively simple; a large gap to humans remained on complex WebArena tasks.
- Launched in Operator for US Pro users; API access planned.
- OpenAI did not expect reliable performance in all scenarios yet.
