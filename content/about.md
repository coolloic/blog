Hi, I'm Loic, an engineering lead and senior software engineer based in New Zealand. I've been building software for 18 years, mostly with React, TypeScript, Node.js and AWS, and before that with Java and Spring.

Most recently I spent four years at Datarock building a cloud-native platform for visualising machine-learning rock classification results. There I led the move from DynamoDB to PostgreSQL and built tooling that let new services start with consistent AWS infrastructure. Before that I worked on:

- **Banking:** cut Mobile Internet Banking login at Bank of New Zealand from about 800ms to 300ms.
- **Energy:** built billing and offer services on AWS serverless for the Mercury app.
- **Telecommunications:** made network maps of thousands of nodes interactive at Aviat Networks, and moved a live AngularJS app to Vue without a rewrite.
- **Regulated documents:** tracked down a page-load problem in a deeply nested document editor at Docuvera.

The work I enjoy most is finding where the time actually goes, and helping a team change a live system without stopping it.

I write here about AI engineering: how models, agents and the tools around them really work, and what I learn building with them. The [wiki](wiki/_index.md) is my running knowledge base on the subject.

For my full experience, skills and certifications, see [my CV](https://coolloic.github.io/).
